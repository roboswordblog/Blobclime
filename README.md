# Blobclime
### Type: Multiplayer Verticle Platformer
### Goal: Score the highest before the game ends

The game ends when a player makes it to the end or the timer runs out.

The 2 ways to get score is to collect score coins(10 points each) or reach the end (100 points for being first).

#### (Not decided debating featuere 1)

You can get weapons to fight and damage your opponent.
To get these weapons you have to go to the shop and use your coins. 
To get these coins, you have to collect them across the map.
You can also do knockback to hit your opponent off.
Upon a players health hitting 0, they go to the bottom and have to wait for 3 seconds, giving the opponent and advantige.

#### (Not decided debating feature 2)

When you run into a player, your velocity stops and transfers into the player, giving them your velcoity but smaller.
This effectively means that you can bump players off.
There is no hp system, and you can't die. 
You can only be pushed off.
You get trophies by winning levels. 
Trophies can allow you to give you buffs, and are a sign of your status.
